1 The Brief

Off Streets On Sports produce bespoke sportswear for teams, clubs, and individuals — but their ordering process relied entirely on back-and-forth conversations, with no online presence to drive enquiries or showcase what they could produce.

The brief was to build a website that not only looked the part for a premium sportswear brand, but gave visitors a tangible way to engage with the product — a kit builder where teams could start designing their apparel and submit it as an order enquiry.

The site needed to feel bold and confident — matching the energy of the brand — while being straightforward enough that a grassroots football club could pick it up and use it without hand-holding.

2 What I Built

  • Bespoke website designed to match the bold visual identity of the brand — built from scratch, not a template
  • Bespoke kit builder — teams can choose their sport, colours, and style, then submit their design as an order enquiry directly through the site
  • Product showcase with high-quality imagery presenting the full range of kit styles and customisation options
  • Enquiry and contact system tied into the kit builder — orders feed directly to the client without the customer leaving the site
  • Fully responsive across all devices — easy to use whether a club manager is on a phone or desktop
  • SEO foundation and managed hosting — the site is fast, secure, and visible to teams searching for custom kit

The Result

A branded platform that turns browsing teams into paying customers.

Off Streets On Sports now has a platform that does their selling for them. Teams land on the site, explore the kit range, build their design, and submit an enquiry — all without a single back-and-forth email before the conversation even begins. The site positions the brand as professional and established, which is exactly what a team captain needs to see before committing to a kit order.
