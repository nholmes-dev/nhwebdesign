1 The Brief

Preston Autocare had built a solid reputation locally through word-of-mouth over the years — but had no online presence to show for it. When potential customers searched for a local garage, they simply couldn't find them.

The brief was clear: a professional website that reflected the quality and reliability of the business, made it easy for customers to find their services, and gave people the confidence to pick up the phone before they'd even visited.

It needed to be fast, mobile-friendly, and built with local search in mind — putting Preston Autocare in front of people actively looking for a garage in the area.

2 What I Built

  • Bespoke website — designed and built from scratch, no templates, no page builders
  • Clear services layout — covering all offerings including MOTs, servicing, repairs, and diagnostics
  • Click-to-call & contact form — customers can reach the garage in one tap on mobile
  • Google Reviews widget — real customer reviews displayed on the site to build trust immediately
  • Local SEO foundation — structured data, meta tags, and on-page optimisation targeting local search results
  • Fully responsive & fast — optimised for mobile visitors who are often searching on the go
  • Managed hosting & SSL — the site stays live and up to date without the client lifting a finger

The Result

A professional online presence that works as hard as the garage does.

Preston Autocare now has a website that reflects the quality of the work they do. Customers searching locally can find them, read real reviews, see exactly what services are on offer, and get in touch in seconds — all from a single, well-designed page. The site handles the first impression so the garage can focus on what they do best.
