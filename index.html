About Me

Hi, I'm Nathan — web designer & developer based in Queensbury, Bradford.

I founded NH Web Design with one simple belief: every business deserves a website built specifically for them — not adapted from a template that thousands of others are already using.

I work directly with every client from the very first conversation right through to launch. I take the time to understand your business, your customers, and what you want to achieve online — then I build it properly, from scratch.

Whether you're a local business just getting online for the first time, an established company looking for a professional upgrade, or an entrepreneur with an ambitious idea that needs a custom web application — I have the skills and experience to deliver it.

With expertise across website design, graphic design, branding, and bespoke web development, I can handle every aspect of your online presence under one roof. No agency mark-ups, no account managers in the middle — just straightforward, high-quality work from someone who genuinely cares about the result.

Services

Website Design

No templates here. I design and develop bespoke websites that look and work exactly how you and your customers want them to. Whether you're looking for something simple or have big out-of-the-box ideas, I can help to deliver the perfect website for your business.

Graphic Design

I create personalised website designs that makes your website stand out from the crowd. If you are a new business starting up, then my starter package would suit your needs, including all the basics you need to get started including a beautifully designed logo.

Branding

Do you want the quality of your products and services to be reflected in your brand? I have experience in branding, creating amazing logo designs & producing brand packages, from identity guidelines to corporate stationery. Your website would match the business you're in.

Web Apps

Need something more complex than a standard website? I develop custom, interactive web applications and software tools. Whether it is a dedicated digital scoreboard or a bespoke business system, I can build high-performance solutions tailored specifically to your logic.
